Simitone privacy policy

Made by Oleg, for Oleg, and nobody else. The only account these tools ever access is Oleg's own.

Oleg's data stays on Oleg's own server and in his private repositories. It is never sold, shared, shown to advertisers, or handed to anyone else.

Nobody else is allowed to use these tools, so no one else's data is collected.
